# 🏦 Bank Loan Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end **Bank Loan Analytics Dashboard** built using **SQL Server, Excel, and Power BI** to analyze lending performance, loan portfolio health, borrower behavior, and business KPIs.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Banks require a centralized analytics solution to monitor loan performance, identify lending trends, evaluate portfolio quality, and support data-driven decision-making.

This project analyzes loan applications, funded amounts, repayments, borrower profiles, and credit risk through interactive dashboards.

# 📊 Business Insights

### ✅ Portfolio Performance
- 38.6K loan applications were analyzed.
- The bank funded **$435.76M** and recovered **$473.07M**, indicating strong overall portfolio performance.

### ✅ Good vs Bad Loans
- **86.18%** of loans are classified as Good Loans.
- Only **13.82%** fall under Bad Loans.
- Good Loans generated **$435.8M** in repayments.
- Charged-off loans recovered only **$37.3M** from **$65.5M** funded.

### 📅 Monthly Trend
- Loan applications steadily increased throughout the year.
- December recorded the highest applications, funded amount, and repayments.

### ⏳ Loan Term Analysis
- **36-month loans** account for nearly **73%** of all applications.
- Customers clearly prefer shorter repayment terms.

### 🎯 Loan Purpose
- **Debt Consolidation** is the most common reason for borrowing.
- Credit Card refinancing is the second-largest loan purpose.

### 🏠 Home Ownership
- Customers with **Mortgage** and **Rent** status account for the majority of loan applications.
- Homeowners contribute the smallest share of borrowers.
